About

Hi! I'm Kyjuany, a junior studying Computer Science and Feminist Intersectional Science & Technology Studies at Barnard College. I care a lot about tech justice, AI ethics, tech policy, and algorithmic bias, and I like digging into how data and automated systems actually affect real communities. This page is where I'll post entries and documentation for each project I build in Creative Embedded Systems this semester.

This semester, I want to

Explore the intersections of art and technology, as well as how hardware and software can come together to create creative works.

Get accustomed to documenting my projects and work, creating detailed blog posts and READMEs that allow better insight into the process of creating and not just the final result.

Draw on what inspires and motivates me, possibly combining my interests in data ethics and algorithmic bias into a unique physical representation using all we learn throughout the semester.
