About

Hi! I'm Kyjuany, a junior studying Computer Science and Feminist Intersectional Science & Technology Studies at Barnard College. I care a lot about tech justice, AI ethics, tech policy, and algorithmic bias, and I like digging into how data and automated systems actually affect real communities. This page is where I'll post entries and documentation for each project I build in Creative Embedded Systems this semester.

This semester, I want to

Get hands-on with the ESP32 kit and actually build intuition for circuits, sensors, and wireless communication instead of just reading about them.

Build a habit of documenting as I go, not after the fact, so each module's blog post and README actually capture my process and not just the finished result.

Use the final project to bring my interest in data ethics and algorithmic bias into a physical, embedded system I actually design myself.
